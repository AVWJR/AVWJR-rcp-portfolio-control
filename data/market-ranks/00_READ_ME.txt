Market Ranks — what we have right now (2026-10-05)
=================================================

Internal use only. Not investment advice. Not for LPs until you and counsel say so.

Score date: Nov 1, 2025 (latest point-in-time date the model allows).
Universe: top 200 US metros by Census 5+ unit rental stock (PR excluded).
Formula: five equal pillars (Demand, Demographics, Supply, Affordability, Operating).
Within Supply, 100% weight is on SUP-20 (permits vs jobs). Other Supply measures are weight 0.

Files:
  01_scores_all_200_metros.csv  — Viability Score, exact rank, band, confidence, momentum
  02_data_sources_in_v1.csv     — every input: what it is, source, whether it scores today
  03_top50_scores.csv           — same fields, top 50 only

Limitations you already know:
  - 2-year rent signal held its sign on 2 hold-out dates; not statistically confirmed.
  - Supply pillar alone was negative in the backtest; read supply pressure carefully.
  - No paid data in v1. Local media is commentary only.
